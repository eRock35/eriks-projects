The Challenge:
a new app every other day.

I set myself a goal of shipping one app a week. Then I decided to find out how fast I could really go. Every other morning a brand-new app goes live in the lab. Each one goes after a real business problem and tries to be genuinely fun to use. You try it, you vote keep or kill, and the winners graduate to their own home.

Why do this?

⚡

Speed is a skill

AI has changed how fast one person can go from idea to working product. The only honest way to find the new limit is to push on it every week.

💼

Real problems only

Every app starts from a headache someone actually has at work: slow quotes, awkward sales role-play, meetings nobody prepared for. No toy demos.

🎮

Useful should be fun

Streaks, scoreboards, a bit of theatre. If people don’t come back to a business tool, it isn’t working.

🗳️

You decide what lives

Nothing is precious. Your keep and kill votes decide which apps get a proper launch, and maybe an iPhone app.
