About

I run sales intelligence and customer insights for all of Equifax Workforce Solutions, supporting over $2.7 billion in revenue across more than a dozen verticals. The job is connecting real data insight to business action: applied AI that drives and monitors the business and pulls the signal out of the noise, and custom apps and products that generate revenue on their own.

I've led the charge on deploying and using AI across the organization, and I'm one of the people it's looked to on where the field is going and what is actually worth building.

I lead the teams that build it, and I'm a coach player about it. I work directly with sales to influence live deals and where the business goes next, and I'm in front of the C-suite regularly.

I started at Equifax in 2013 writing code, and I never stopped. Along the way I built the insights and the algorithm that detect synthetic fraud in the credit marketplace, which became two patents, and I've won the company's biggest awards more than once, which is unheard of for someone who isn't a salesperson by trade.

Off the clock: Georgia football, good beer, and planning the next family trip. The apps above exist because I wanted them for myself.
