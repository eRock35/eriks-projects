About

I run sales intelligence for all of Equifax Workforce Solutions, supporting over $2.7 billion in revenue across Commercial, Government, Risk and Public Safety. The job is connecting real data insight to business action: applied AI that drives and monitors the business and pulls the signal out of the noise, and custom apps and products that generate revenue on their own.

I lead the teams that build it, and I'm a coach player about it. I work directly with sales to influence live deals and where the business goes next, and I'm in front of the C-suite regularly.

I started at Equifax in 2013 writing code, and I never stopped. Along the way I built the insights and the algorithm that detect synthetic fraud in the credit marketplace, which became two patents, and I've won the company's biggest awards more than once, which is unheard of for someone who isn't a salesperson by trade.

On the side I run Strong Technical Consulting (Strong & Strong Consulting, L.L.C.), bringing the same thing to small and medium businesses. Off the clock: Georgia football, good beer, and planning the next family trip. The apps above exist because I wanted them for myself.
