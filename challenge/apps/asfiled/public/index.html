Numbers are each company’s own, as tagged in its annual reports filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (EDGAR), and each one links to its filing. A relationship is shown only where a company’s own document names the other company, with the exact words. Nothing here is a forecast. A company outside the reviewed set can be looked up on request: this app’s server then reads it from sec.gov by the same rules, and it is labelled. The page itself makes no request to sec.gov or anywhere else; the links open the sources.
