🔎 Tells

The quick scan is free and works without anything here. A deep read, an originality check and a visual read use your Tells account’s credit, so the extension needs a device key for that account.

  1. Open Tells and sign in.
  2. Settings → Device keys → Make a key. It is shown once.
  3. Paste it here. It stays in this browser’s extension storage and is sent only to Tells.

Pictures on other sites

Tells can read images on LinkedIn and X already. To check a picture anywhere else from the right-click menu, it needs permission to download images from other sites. It only downloads the one you right-click.
