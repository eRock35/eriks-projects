      How the lab works

      1. 1It drops.

        A new app lands here every morning, built around a real problem someone has at work.

      2. 2You play.

        One free account works across every app on the site, with $2 of AI credit to spend however you like.

      3. 3You vote.

        🔥 Keep it or 💀 kill it, then see how the crowd split. Vote on each new drop to keep a streak going. Tell Erik what you’d change.

      4. 4Winners graduate.

        The ones people love get their own address, a proper launch and maybe an iPhone app.

      Built in public by Erik Strong. Everything here is an experiment and may vanish without warning.
