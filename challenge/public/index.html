How the lab works

  1. 1It drops.

    A new app lands here every morning, built around a real problem someone has at work.

  2. 2You play.

    One free account works across every app on the site, with $2 of AI credit to spend however you like.

  3. 3You vote.

    🔥 Keep it or 💀 kill it. Tell Erik what you’d change. Nothing here is precious yet.

  4. 4Winners graduate.

    The ones people love get their own address, a proper launch and maybe an iPhone app.
